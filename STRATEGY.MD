## Observations

Observing the points lookup table, we can observe quite a few important details that will inform our strategy.

1. The relative difference in points awarded between fully completed *N-1* and *N*-stage towers is greatest between 4-stage and 5-stage towers, which reward 30 and 70 points respectively. This is a 40 point jump (133% increase)!

2. The 10-stage tower has the highest ratio of points awarded per stage. The ratio of points awarded per stage in an *N*-stage tower increases until maxing out at the 10-stage tower and decreasing for all subsequent tower sizes.

3. Point rewards for completing the first four stages of any tower are maxed out at the 5-stage and 6-stage towers. Every other tower yields lower rewards for the first four stages. This is due to these towers having a good balance of relatively high point rewards *and* relatively high percentage values in the quadratic equation with fewer rounds completed.

4. The 5-stage and 6-stage tower point rewards are identical for the first four stages, but they split on the fifth stage. These towers reward 70 and 69 points respectively for completing the fifth stage. 

5. The 5-stage tower and 6-stage tower yield 70 and 100 points respectively when fully completed.

6. 18+ stage towers which hit the 300 point cap always reward zero points for completing the first stage, and they do not offer enough points to counterbalance the harsh percentage calculation in the quadratic equation for calculating points. **There is never a scenario where 18+ stage towers are useful for grinding.**

## Optimal Min-Max Strategy

The optimal min-max strategy is entirely dependent on two factors: the **amount of stage** you can consistently complete per tower, and **how quickly** you can do it.

1. If you consistently complete four stages or less, then you should always grind in a 5-stage or 6-stage tower. These towers yield the highest point rewards for the first four rounds. 

2. If you consistently complete five to six stages, then you should grind in a 6-stage tower. While 6-stage towers do reduce your fifth stage completion reward by 1 point, they increase your potential total reward by 30 points compared to the 5-stage tower. 

3. If you consistently complete greater than six stages, then you should calculate the amount of time it takes you to do that. For instance, if you can consistently complete seven stages and it only takes you an extra thirty seconds, then it makes sense for you to grind on a 7-stage tower. However, if you take four minutes to make it to the last stage of a 7-stage tower due to retries or hesitation, then it is generally better to just complete 6-stage towers completely instead.

If you are playing on a public server with the 6-stage noob towers and you consistently complete six stages or less, then congrats: you are already using this min-max strategy! Contrary to what you might think, grinding smaller 4-stage towers or larger 10+ stage towers is never an efficient way to grind.

If you have a private server available to you, take advantage of the skip feature to skip towers with incredibly difficult early stages!
